# Find your way around

A one-minute map of Deck: the sidebar areas, how to open Deck, search, and hiding what you don't use.

## The areas

The sidebar lists Deck's areas. Click one to open its first page; the row of tabs above the page (**Pages in <area>**) holds the others. Below 1000 px wide the sidebar shows only icons (hover one for its name); **Settings › General › Sidebar style** changes that.

Moving around:

- Up/Down, Home and End move between areas; Enter opens one.
- Alt+Up/Down moves an area, Alt+Left/Right moves a page tab. **Arrange** in Settings › General lists them all ([Sidebar](settings.md#sidebar)).
- After a link takes you to another page, **Back to <page>** appears at the start of the tab row (**Back to results** after a search). The mouse Back button and Alt+Left do the same.

## Open Deck

- Rest the pointer in a hot corner (top left by default),
- click the Deck icon in the tray next to the clock, or
- press your keyboard shortcut, if you set one.

The **X** only hides Deck; it keeps running in the tray. Choose **Quit Deck** in the tray menu to quit. More in [Home](home.md#opening-and-closing-deck).

## Search

Type in the search box at the top of any page. Search finds pages, tabs, settings, apps, games, files and commands. It ignores capitals and accents, accepts words in any order and forgives one wrong letter in longer words. Each result shows where it lives (**Group › Page › Tab**); choosing a setting opens its page and lights up the row.

With the box empty, search shows your **Pinned** and **Recent** pages. Pin a result with its pin button. See [Launcher and search](launcher.md).

## Show only what you use

**Settings › Features** hides whole areas or single pages from the sidebar, search, Home and the tray menu. Hiding never stops or deletes anything. **Show everything** brings it all back. See [Features](settings.md#features).

## See what Deck has changed

The **Activity** button next to the search box counts the changes Deck is holding (a fan setting, a game mode). Open it for **What Deck changed**, with **Undo** for each change. See [What Deck changed](home.md#what-deck-changed). To hide the button, turn off **Show Activity** in [Settings › Appearance](settings.md#appearance).

## Page tours

The **Show me around this page** button next to the search box plays a short tour of the page. Tours never start on their own.
