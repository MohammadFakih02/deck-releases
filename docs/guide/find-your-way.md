# Find your way around

This page is a map of Deck in one minute: the areas in the sidebar, how to open Deck, how to find anything with search, and how to hide the areas you do not use.

## The areas

The sidebar on the left lists Deck's areas. Click one and it opens its first page. The row of tabs above the page, labelled **Pages in <area>**, lists the other pages of that area. Every page has a title and one sentence under it that says what the page is for.

| Area | What it is for |
|---|---|
| **Home** | Your starting board: quick controls, live readings, the game you are playing, and the first steps for a new install. |
| **Gaming** | What changes on your PC while a game runs: [Game profiles](gaming.md), the [Performance overlay](overlay.md), the [Controller](controller.md) overlay, the [Death counter](death-counter.md) and the [Game bar](game-bar.md). |
| **Hardware** | How your PC runs: [CPU](cpu.md), [GPU](gpu.md), [Fans](fans.md), [Lighting](lighting.md) and the Cooler display. |
| **PC status** | What your PC is doing: [Sensors](sensors.md), [Processes](processes.md), [Network](network.md), [Drive health](drives.md) and [This PC](this-pc.md). |
| **Desktop** | Everyday tools: [Arrange windows](windows.md), [Display](display.md), [Sound & mic](sound.md), [Music & volume pop-ups](flyouts.md), [Clipboard](clipboard.md) and Screen tools. |
| **Maintenance** | Keeping Windows tidy: [Clean up](cleanup.md), Disk space, [Apps](apps.md), [Startup apps](startup.md), the Startup optimizer and [Windows tweaks](tweaks.md). |
| **Power & time** | [Keep awake, Timers & breaks and Shut down when…](power.md). |

Under the areas, always visible: [PC to PC](pc-to-pc.md), [Project sync](workspace-sync.md) (only while it is on), [Phone](phone.md) and [Settings](settings.md).

Pages for hardware your PC does not have are hidden. [This PC](this-pc.md) lists what Deck found.

Switches show **On** or **Off** beside the control. Where a row already spells out the state, it keeps that fuller wording, such as Phone's **On: phones can connect**. Page titles stay on one line, with the search row moving underneath when needed; hover a shortened title to read it in full.

## Open Deck

- Rest the pointer in the hot corner (top left by default), or
- click the Deck icon in the tray next to the clock, or
- press your keyboard shortcut, if you set one.

Closing Deck with the **X** only hides it. It keeps running in the tray. Choose **Quit Deck** in the tray menu to quit. More in [Home](home.md#opening-and-closing-deck).

The tray menu is short: **Open Deck**, **Open Clipboard**, **Keep the PC awake**, **Settings**, **What Deck changed**, then three groups, **Gaming**, **Hardware** and **Desktop**, and **Quit Deck**.

## Search

Open Deck, type in the search box at the top and press Enter. Search finds every page, every tab of a page and every control or setting on them, as well as apps, games, files and commands. It ignores capitals and accents, accepts words in any order and forgives a single wrong letter in longer words. Choosing a setting opens its page and lights the row for a moment. Old names still work: typing "Overlay" finds **Performance overlay**. See [Launcher and search](launcher.md).

Search takes focus when you click it or start typing outside another field or a dialogue. Opening Deck or changing page does not focus it automatically.

## Show only what you use

Open **Settings › Features** to hide whole areas or single pages from the sidebar, search, Home and the tray menu. Hiding only removes the entrance. It never stops anything that is running or deletes anything. **Show everything** brings it all back. See [Settings](settings.md#features).

## See what Deck has changed on your PC

When Deck is changing something on your PC (a fan setting, a game mode), a chip at the top of the page says **Deck is changing 2 things**. Open it to see **What Deck changed**, with a **Put back** button for each change. See [Home](home.md#what-deck-changed). To hide the chip, turn off **Show what Deck is changing** in [Settings › Appearance](settings.md#appearance); the tray menu still has **What Deck changed**.

## Learning a page

Press the tour button next to the search box to play a short tour of the page you are on. Tours never start by themselves.
